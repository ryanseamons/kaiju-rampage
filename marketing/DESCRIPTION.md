You hatch in Shiokaze Bay as **TIDEMAW**, a lizard about the size of a van. Eat your way up to a city-ending monster while the army throws everything it has at you.

**Crush the city to grow. Beat the army to level up.** Anything in your size class falls when you touch it: cars and trees at first, then houses, then towers and tanks. Each of the three size tiers zooms the camera out and changes how the military fights. Infantry advance at first. Later they panic and the tanks take over. In the end, artillery shells you from across the city.

- **5 waves, about 10 minutes.** The last wave ends when you take down the giant mech M-01 Shiokaze Guardian.
- **Mutations** on every level-up: atomic breath, tail sweeps, spine volleys, a shock aura and more.
- **Breaking news** between waves: the city's news desk reports on *your* run, including what you flattened, where it was hit hardest and how close you came to dying.
- **Landmarks** worth big points: Shiokaze Castle, the KBN-7 tower, and the Old Town pagodas and torii gates.
- **Easy, Medium and Hard**, a high-score table, and a soundtrack of 8-bit electric guitar.

**Controls:** WASD or the arrow keys to move. Your claws attack on their own. Space stomps, P pauses and M mutes. Gamepads work too.

*Kaiju* (怪獣) is Japanese for "strange beast". The Japanese title, 怪獣大暴れ, means "the kaiju's great rampage".
